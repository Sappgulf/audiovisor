Privacy policy for the Audiovisor iPhone and iPad app.

Effective November 17, 2026

Audio stays with you

Audiovisor analyzes imported audio on your device to create visuals. It does not upload your audio, library, listening history or visual settings to us. No account is required.

What the app stores

When you import from Files or iCloud Drive, the app keeps a private copy in its own storage. It also stores your visual preferences on the device. Removing a track from My music deletes the app’s copy; it does not remove your original file. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to your device’s backup and restore settings.

No advertising or tracking

The iOS app contains no advertising, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking. We do not collect personal data through the app. Apple may process diagnostics, downloads and purchase-related account information under your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

Files, iCloud and external links

The system file picker gives the app access to audio files you choose. If you select a file in iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider handles downloading it under its own policies. Core playback and visuals work offline after import. If you open our website or email support, that service handles the connection and any information you choose to send. Optional streaming integrations on the separate web app are not part of the iOS app.

Support and changes

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you send so we can respond. Please do not send audio files or sensitive information unless needed to resolve your request. We will update this page if the app’s privacy practices change.
