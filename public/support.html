Help for Audiovisor on iPhone and iPad.

Contact support

Start a session

On Discover, tap Play the demo to try the built-in track. Tap Import your music to choose audio from Files or iCloud Drive. Imported tracks appear in My music and remain available offline.

Choose a visual world

Tap the grid on the visual preview or View all to browse 24 visuals. Choose a world, then open the stage. Tap the visual name on the stage to change visuals, color themes and audio settings. Double-tap the stage to enter or leave immersive mode. Tap Studio to return home.

A track won’t import or play

Use an unprotected MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, AAC, AIFF, OGG or Opus file under 100 MB. Codec support varies by device and file. Protected downloads from streaming subscriptions cannot be imported. If a file is in iCloud Drive, download it in Files first and try again.

No sound?

Check your device volume and selected audio output, then pause and resume. If the stage cannot load, use Retry or App information → Reload visual engine. Reloading stops the current session but keeps your imported library.

Manage your library

Use the track’s options menu in My music to remove its imported copy. Your original in Files is unchanged. Local data may be included in your device backups according to your Apple settings.

Tell us what happened

Email sappgulf@gmail.com with your app version, device model, iOS version and steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots help. Please avoid sending private audio or other sensitive information.
